## #1 — Fairy-Architecture HEC (daily driver)

**Premise:** rebuilds Fairy's working surfactant architecture (SLES + CAPB + LDAO) from raw materials at Pasquet 2022's measured 10 % glycerin peak and Flavour Blaster's commercial HEC level. Free-running pH (Pasquet measured neutralising hurts). Robust, longest shelf life of the three, hardest to mess up.

### Ingredients (1 L batch, by mass)

| # | Ingredient | g per 1 L | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Distilled water | 875 | Base |
| 2 | Vegetable glycerin USP | 100 | Humectant (Pasquet 140× peak at 10 %) |
| 3 | Disodium EDTA | 0.5 | Chelator (log K = 10.7 with Ca²⁺) |
| 4 | HEC Cellosize QP-100MH | 2.2 | Polymer (matches Flavour Blaster INCI) |
| 5 | SLES-70 paste | 20 | Anionic surfactant (1.4 % active) |
| 6 | CAPB-30 | 15 | Amphoteric synergy (0.45 % active) |
| 7 | LDAO-30 / Ammonyx LO | 5 | Non-ionic foam booster (0.15 % active) |

**pH:** free-running 7.5-8.0. **Don't adjust.**

**Total surfactant actives:** 2.0 % (slight margin above Pasquet's 1.5 % ceiling for wand uptake).

### Mix

1. Slurry 2.2 g HEC into 100 g glycerin in a 250 mL beaker, stirring with a dry spatula until polymer is wetted. The glycerin pre-coats the polymer so it doesn't fish-eye when water hits it.
2. In a 2 L bucket: 875 g distilled water + 0.5 g disodium EDTA, stir until optically clear.
3. **Slow paddle** the water-EDTA. Pour the HEC/glycerin slurry into the vortex centre over 30 s. Continue gentle paddle 5 min. Cover. **Rest undisturbed 12-24 hr.**
4. After rest, slow paddle. Add in this order: **20 g SLES-70** over 30 s, then **15 g CAPB-30** over 30 s, then **5 g LDAO-30** over 30 s. Fold gently 5 min. Don't whip — no foam in the bucket.
5. Cover, rest 24-48 hr cool dark. Peak performance T+48-72 hr from initial mix. Shelf life 2-6 weeks.

### Use when

Calm to moderate wind. Default. The "always have a bucket of this" recipe.

## #2 — AOS Wind-Tolerant (Scotland-proof)

**Premise:** pure-repulsion film mechanism. AOS sulfonate + APAM polyelectrolyte mesh at neutral pH = no CAPB coacervate, no shear-melt failure mode. Bergeron 1996 + von Klitzing 2002 + Yekeen 2018 measured this architecture in EOR and fluorine-free firefighting foam. Science-backed answer to "huge in calm, dead in wind."

### Ingredients (1 L batch, by mass)

| # | Ingredient | g per 1 L | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Distilled water | 855 | Base (835 g to bucket + 20 g for J-Lube slurry) |
| 2 | Vegetable glycerin USP | 70 | Humectant (lower than #1 — polymer mesh handles film thickness) |
| 3 | Tetrasodium EDTA | 1.0 | Chelator (higher dose because two polymers need protection) |
| 4 | APAM / HPAM 10-20 MDa (Flopaam 3330S or 3430S) | 0.15 | Polyelectrolyte mesh — repulsion network builder |
| 5 | J-Lube (PEO + sucrose, polydisperse) | 0.4 | Backup polymer with polydispersity to 8 MDa |
| 6 | AOS-40 (sodium C14-16 olefin sulfonate) | 60 | Primary anionic (2.4 % active) |
| 7 | LDAO-30 (optional) | 5 | Mild foam stabiliser (0.15 % active) |
| 8 | **CAPB** | **0** | **Deliberately omitted — coacervate eliminator** |

**pH:** free-running 7.5-8.0. **No buffer, no acid.**

**Total surfactant actives:** 2.55 %. Above Pasquet ceiling — accepted trade-off for the repulsion-network architecture which depends on AOS micelle density.

### Mix (slow paddle ONLY — no blender, ever; high shear scissions APAM)

1. Slurry 0.15 g APAM into 70 g glycerin in a 250 mL beaker, dry-spatula stir. APAM won't dissolve in glycerin alone — just disperse. Cover.
2. In a 100 mL beaker, slurry 0.4 g J-Lube into 20 g of the distilled water (taken from the 855 g total). Stir gently 30 s. Cover.
3. In a 2 L bucket: 835 g water (remaining) + 1.0 g tetrasodium EDTA. Stir until optically clear.
4. **Slow paddle the bucket.** Pour the APAM/glycerin slurry into the vortex centre over 30 s. Fold gently 5 min. Cover. **Rest 6 hr.**
5. After 6 hr, slow paddle. Pour the J-Lube/water slurry into the centre over 30 s. Fold gently 5 min. Cover. **Rest 6 hr more.**
6. After 12 hr total polymer hydration, slow paddle. Add **60 g AOS-40** over 30 s, then **5 g LDAO-30** over 30 s. Fold gently 5 min. Mix should be optically clear, very pale straw, no foam in the bucket.
7. Cover, rest 48 hr cool dark. Peak performance T+48-72 hr. Shelf life 2 weeks.

### Use when

Any wind over ~8 mph. Outdoor gusty sessions. Anywhere the Fairy-architecture HEC mainline starts breaking up. This is your wind recipe.

## #3 — 816 g Corrected (your 100 ft recipe, fixed)

**Premise:** your original 816 g recipe (SLES+CAPB+LDAO + PAM + PEO at pH 6.1 with sorbic acid + citric chelation) already produced 100 ft / 200 ft bubbles in calm air. Three things broke it: sorbic-acid pH lock at 6.1 created CAPB-SLES coacervate that shear-melts in wind, total surfactant 3 % exceeded Pasquet's measured 1.5 % ceiling, citric chelation phase-split the mix. This rescue version keeps the dual-polymer (your known-working part) but lifts pH out of the coacervate zone, drops surfactant to 2 %, and uses EDTA only.

### Ingredients (1 L batch, by mass)

| # | Ingredient | g per 1 L | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Distilled water | 850 | Base |
| 2 | Vegetable glycerin USP | 100 | Humectant |
| 3 | Disodium EDTA | 0.5 | Chelator (replaces citric acid — see [[10-acid-titration-phase-split]]) |
| 4 | PEO WSR-301 (4 MDa, fresh) | 0.20 | Polydisperse film backbone |
| 5 | APAM / HPAM 10-20 MDa | 0.05 | Trace polyelectrolyte — extends relaxation spectrum |
| 6 | SLES-70 paste | 20 | Anionic (1.4 % active) |
| 7 | CAPB-30 | 12 | Amphoteric (0.36 % active — slightly under #1 because dual polymer carries more load) |
| 8 | LDAO-30 | 5 | Non-ionic (0.15 % active) |
| 9 | **Sorbic acid / citric acid / DKP** | **0** | **All removed.** Caused coacervate lock and phase split. |

**pH:** free-running 7.5-8.0. **No acid, no buffer.** Your old recipe locked at 6.1 was the coacervate zone.

**Total surfactant actives:** 1.91 % (down from your original 3 %).

### Mix

1. Dry polymer blend: 0.20 g PEO + 0.05 g APAM in a small beaker, stir 30 s dry to disperse.
2. Slurry the dry polymer blend into 100 g glycerin. Stir with a dry spatula 2 min until uniformly dispersed (a faint cloudy gel forms).
3. In a 2 L bucket: 850 g distilled water + 0.5 g disodium EDTA. Stir until clear.
4. **Slow paddle only — no blender, no high shear.** Pour polymer/glycerin slurry into the vortex centre over 30 s. Fold 5 min. Cover. **Rest 12 hr.**
5. Slow paddle. Add 20 g SLES-70, then 12 g CAPB-30, then 5 g LDAO-30, each over 30 s. Fold 5 min.
6. Cover, rest 24-48 hr cool dark. Shelf life 2-3 weeks.

### Use when

Calm air, hunting personal records. This is the closest evolution of your previous best recipe — same architecture, three specific science-based fixes applied.

## Three rules that apply to all three

1. **Distilled water only.** UK tap water (especially England) carries enough Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ to crash polymer behaviour. No exceptions.
2. **EDTA for chelation. Period.** No citric, no lactic, no sorbic acid. See [[10-acid-titration-phase-split]] for why citric chelation phase-splits mixes overnight.
3. **Slow paddle, never a blender.** High shear scissions long polymer chains — exactly the chains that make giant bubbles giant. Patience > speed.
